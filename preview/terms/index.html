Terms and conditions

Last updated 29 September 2026.

Who may register

Registration is open only to bishops and pastors of the organizations served by Kuriake Castle, using the sign-up link provided by the office or by your bishop. Creating a record for anyone other than yourself is not permitted.

Accuracy

You confirm that the details you give are true and that the photograph is of you, taken in official attire. Bishops confirm that the pastors on their list are under their oversight. The office may request a resubmission or decline a registration.

Annual renewal fee

The annual renewal ministerial fee is non-refundable once paid. It covers the registration year shown at the time of payment.

The public roll

By registering you agree to the publication described in the privacy policy: name, title, photograph, organization, denomination, city and country. You may ask for your record to be withdrawn at any time.

Accounts

Keep your email account secure; anyone who can read your email can request a sign-in code. The office may remove accounts that are duplicated, abandoned or misused.

Changes

We may update these terms and the privacy policy. The date at the top tells you when the text last changed.

Contact

Use the "Any issues?" button on the site, or write to the Kuriake Castle office.
