2:T92f,<h1>Privacy policy</h1>
<p>Last updated 29 September 2026.</p>
<p>Kuriake Castle (kuriakecastle.org) keeps the annual Roll of Good Standing for the bishops and pastors of its member organizations. This policy explains what we collect, what is public, and what is not.</p>
<h2>What we collect</h2>
<p>When you register we ask for your name, gender, role, organization and denomination, city and country, a photograph in official attire, your email address, your phone number and your date of birth. Bishops also give us the names and dates of birth of the pastors under their oversight. The annual fee is paid by mobile money to the Kuriake Castle account; we keep the Transaction ID and the screenshot you upload so the office can confirm it against the statement.</p>
<h2>What is public</h2>
<p>The public Roll of Good Standing shows your <strong>name, title, photograph, organization, denomination, city and country</strong>, and, for pastors, the bishop you serve under. Nothing else about you is public.</p>
<h2>What is never public</h2>
<p>Your <strong>email address, phone number and date of birth</strong> are never shown on the public site. They are used only by the Kuriake Castle office and by your own bishop to confirm that you are who you say you are, to match you to your bishop's list, and to contact you about your registration.</p>
<h2>Your consent</h2>
<p>You are asked to consent to this before your registration is submitted. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to the office; we will remove your record from the public roll.</p>
<h2>How long we keep it</h2>
<p>Registrations are kept for each year so that the roll has a history. You may ask the office to delete your account and all of its data at any time.</p>
<h2>Security</h2>
<p>Data is stored in a database in the European Union with encrypted connections. Sign-in uses one-time codes sent to your email. Photographs are re-encoded and stripped of location and device metadata on upload.</p>
<h2>Third parties</h2>
<p>We use Hostinger (hosting), Resend (email delivery) and, for card or mobile-money checkout, Paystack (payments). Each receives only what it needs to do its job. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.</p>

<p>Questions or requests: use the "Any issues?" button on the site, or write to the Kuriake Castle office.</p>7:X
